Learning the games

Reporting a problem

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The app version or TestFlight build.
  • Which game and whether you were playing normally or in practice.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to it.
  • A screenshot if useful. Remove personal information before sending.

TestFlight testers can also send feedback through TestFlight. Please tell us about small cards, unclear instructions, overlapping text, incorrect payouts, or hands that are difficult to understand.

Saved progress

Your bankroll, settings, and saved games stay on your device. Finish an active hand before changing tables. Deleting the app can remove local progress, so contact support before deleting it to troubleshoot.
