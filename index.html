A little help with your Sudoku.

Questions, feedback, or something not working? Contact George Algozzini, the independent developer of Sudoku My Way.

sudokumywaysupport@icloud.com

Reporting a problem

Please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The app version or TestFlight build, if you know it.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to it.
  • A screenshot, if helpful. Crop out personal information first.

For a confusing hint, include the board and the hint explanation. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Learning and hints

Open How to Play on the title screen for the basics. During a game, Hint explains one logical step on the board. A hint may remove a pencil-mark candidate rather than fill in a number.

Your records and saved game

Statistics shows one difficulty at a time. You can reset that difficulty or choose Reset All Statistics to clear every difficulty’s records. A confirmation is required. These actions do not clear your settings or saved game.

Your progress is stored on your device. Please contact support before deleting the app to troubleshoot a problem, because deleting it can remove your saved progress.
